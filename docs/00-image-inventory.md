## Notes on the inventory

- Images 3–12 are the **10 step modules** ("10 modul gambar" in the brief). Images 1–2 are overview/roadmap posters.
- Image 6 uses a **churn** example and image 7/12 use `fraud-model`. No image mentions credit scoring, PSI/CSI for scorecards, KS/Gini, OJK, PSAK 71, Basel, UU PDP, SHAP, or CI/CD for ML. Those are added in this syllabus and labeled `[TAMBAHAN]`.
- Image 7 and 12 use `client.transition_model_version_stage(...)`. `[TAMBAHAN]` In MLflow ≥ 2.9 registry *stages* are deprecated in favor of **aliases** (`set_registered_model_alias`) and tags. The syllabus teaches aliases and maps them to the image's stage names.
- Footer elements (logo, social icons, QR codes) carry no MLOps content and are ignored.
